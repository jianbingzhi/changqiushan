## Brand & Style
The brand personality is rooted in the stewardship of the natural world, balancing environmental preservation with professional administrative rigor. The design system evokes a sense of calm, reliability, and organic precision. 

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with a Biophilic influence**. It utilizes a clean, systematic layout characteristic of high-end SaaS platforms but softens the edges with a nature-inspired palette and subtle organic depth. The goal is to provide a stress-free environment for park rangers and administrators to manage complex ecological data and park operations.

## Colors
The palette is dominated by **Evergreen (#2D5A27)**, used for primary actions, navigation headers, and critical status indicators to signify authority and the forest environment. 

**Earthy Brown (#8B5E34)** serves as the secondary accent for secondary buttons and trail-related categorization, while **Olive Moss (#6B8E23)** is used for success states and ecological health indicators. The background uses a cool-toned **Off-White (#F9FAFB)** to reduce eye strain during long administrative sessions, with **Pure White (#FFFFFF)** reserved for content containers to create clear structural separation.

## Typography
This design system utilizes **Inter** for its exceptional legibility in data-dense interfaces and its neutral, modern character. 

The scale is built for hierarchy: Large display titles are used for dashboard overviews, while a robust set of "Body" and "Label" styles handle the complex tables and forms required for forest management. Letter spacing is slightly tightened on headlines for a more "locked-in" professional feel, while labels use increased tracking and uppercase styling to differentiate metadata from primary content.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **Fluid Grid** model with a 12-column structure for desktop. To maintain a professional and organized feel, all spacing is derived from a **8px base unit**.

- **Desktop:** 40px outer margins with 24px gutters.
- **Tablet:** 24px outer margins with 16px gutters.
- **Mobile:** 16px outer margins.

Content is organized into "Zonal Blocks" where related management tools are grouped in white surface cards. Vertical rhythm is strictly enforced to ensure tabular data remains scannable.

## Elevation & Depth
The system uses **Tonal Layering** combined with **Ambient Shadows** to create a sense of organized depth without overwhelming the user.

- **Level 0 (Background):** #F9FAFB. Used for the canvas.
- **Level 1 (Cards/Containers):** #FFFFFF. Uses a very soft, diffused shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.05)`. Note the subtle primary color tint in the shadow to maintain the organic feel.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns/Modals):** #FFFFFF. Uses a deeper shadow: `0px 10px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Buttons):** Uses a high-contrast shadow to indicate interactivity.

Borders are used sparingly, primarily as 1px strokes in a light grey-green (#E5E7EB) to define table rows and input fields.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Rounded**, reflecting the soft curves found in nature while maintaining the structural integrity of a professional system.

- **Standard (8px):** Buttons, Input Fields, and Cards.
- **Large (16px):** Featured dashboard widgets and imagery containers.
- **Pill:** Status tags (e.g., "Active", "Maintenance Required") and filter chips.

This consistent radius ensures the UI feels approachable and modern, moving away from the harsh corners of traditional legacy management software.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons use a solid **Evergreen** fill with white text. Secondary buttons use an **Earthy Brown** outline or a ghost style with Evergreen text. Hover states should involve a subtle darkening of the fill or a light Evergreen background tint for ghost buttons.

### Input Fields
Inputs feature an 8px corner radius and a 1px border (#D1D5DB). Upon focus, the border transitions to Evergreen with a 2px outer glow (soft green).

### Data Tables
Tables are the heart of the system. Rows use a 48px height for readability, with a subtle 1px bottom border. Header rows are slightly tinted (#F3F4F6) with uppercase bold labels.

### Status Chips
Used for park alerts (Fire Risk, Weather, Trail Status). These are pill-shaped with low-opacity background fills matching their status color (e.g., Red for High Risk, Olive for Safe) and high-contrast text.

### Interactive Maps
Integrated map components should use custom styling to match the evergreen palette, with custom markers for ranger stations, fire towers, and trailheads.
